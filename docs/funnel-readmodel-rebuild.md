# Funnel Readmodel Rebuild

Funnel observations are immutable and exactly attributed to workspace, content item, revision, target, attempt, source, and campaign. Corrections are separate records.

Readmodels are derived and rebuildable: content funnels, campaign funnels, channel comparisons, revision comparisons, attribution quality, and reconciliation operations. Failed or incomplete builds are not marked current, and the previous current readmodel remains readable.
