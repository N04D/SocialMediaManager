# Analytics Framework

Owned-publication funnel metrics stay content-aware: website, social, CTA, and conversion observations are tied to content revision, publication target, campaign, and attribution ID. Incomplete attribution is visible instead of hidden.

Website observations such as page views, engaged visits, CTA clicks, conversions, and conversion value are attributed to the exact content revision, website target, publication attempt, campaign, and optional source social target.

Funnel readmodels distinguish social engagement, link clicks, website landing, engaged reading, CTA, and conversion. They do not claim causality from correlation alone.
