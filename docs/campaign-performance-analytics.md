# Campaign Performance Analytics

Campaign analytics uses concrete historical publications, not planned targets.

It includes publication count, completed occurrence context, uncertain publication markers, channel breakdown, account breakdown, content breakdown, compatible metrics, channel-specific metrics, freshness, completeness, and warnings.

Cancelled or never-executed targets do not count as performance. Campaign membership is a dimension and does not duplicate publications.

No causal claim is made that a campaign, time slot, or media asset caused performance changes.
